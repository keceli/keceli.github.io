\resheading{Contributions to the Argonne Leadership Computing Facility}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Catalyst for ADSP, ESP, and INCITE Projects:} Facilitating the adoption and efficient use of ALCF resources for computational chemistry, material science, and machine learning applications.
    \item \textbf{INCITE, ALCC, DD  Allocations:} Helping teams to write compelling proposals for computer time at ALCF resources. Reviewing INCITE applications annually for computational readiness since 2018 and participated as an observer in the INCITE science panel in 2023.
    \item \textbf{ALCF Workshop Organization:} Contributed to organizational decisions, reviewed participant applications, and coordinated with experts to serve as speakers for the first in-person workshop after pandemic. Currently serving on the organizing committee of the INCITE Hackathon workshop in May 2024.
    \item \textbf{Contributor to ALCF Documentation:} Contributing to the online documentation to improve accessibility and understanding of ALCF's computational resources. Working on a question and answer bot based on large language models that can help new users.
    \item \textbf{Advocate for interactive high-performance computing:} Promoted the integration and efficient use of Jupyter notebooks for HPC applications as highlighted in a recent ALCF interview. This interview was covered in \href{https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/argonnes-murat-keceli-helps-prepare-jupyter-notebooks-for-aurora/}{HPCWire}.
    \item \textbf{ATPESC Review Committees Member:} Participating in the annual review process for the Argonne Training Program on Extreme-Scale Computing since 2018.
    \item \textbf{Porting and Optimizing codes for Polaris and Aurora:} Actively working on porting, testing, and optimizing critical computational chemistry codes and machine learning frameworks for Polaris and Aurora.
    \item \textbf{Presenter at ALCF Workshops and Developer Sessions:} Prepared and presented talks for ALCF workshops on container technologies at ALCF. Presented webinars on utilizing ALCF JupyterHub for interactive computing in 2021 and 2023.
    \item \textbf{Outreach activities for HPC:} 
    Volunteered as a mentor or presenter in various activities to introduce HPC in underrepresented communities such as Hour of Code, Sustainable Research Pathways program, and Introduction to HPC Bootcamp.
\end{itemize}
