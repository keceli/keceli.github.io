\ressubheading{}{}{\bf Quantum chemistry at UIUC and UF} {2007 - 2012}
\begin{itemize}
\resitem{Developed a general scheme to calculate anharmonic vibrational frequencies and vibrationally-averaged structures of molecules at the complete-correlation complete-basis-set limit using a novel hybrid potential energy surface representation that combines the merits of grid and Taylor expansion based representations.}
%\resitem{Derived the formulations of size-extensive vibrational self-consistent field, M\o ller--Plesset perturbation, and coupled-cluster methods for anharmonic lattice vibrations of extended periodic systems on the basis of a quartic force field in normal coordinates.}
\resitem{Utilized coupled-cluster singles and doubles with a noniterative triples [CCSD(T)] correction for solid hydrogen fluoride by employing a type of fragment molecular orbital (FMO) method based on a truncated many-body expansion of the energies of molecular crystals.}
\resitem{Introduced the mod-$n$ approximation (uniform downsampling of the wave vectors in Brillouin-zone integrations) for crystal-orbital coupled-cluster singles and doubles (CCSD) method that reduces the computational cost more than 500 times.}
\resitem{Invented the diagonalization-free size-extensive vibrational self-consistent field (XVSCF) method for molecules, which is the fastest variational approach to include anharmonicity in vibrational calculations.}
\resitem{Wrote a standalone computer program (Fortran 95) that can generate a quartic force field potential energy surface and solve the vibrational Schr\"{o}dinger equation based on Watson Hamiltonian. Implemented methods are: VSCF, virtual VSCF, VPT2, VMP2,  HO-VCI, VSCF-VCI, XVSCF, XVPT2, and XVMP2.}
