Scientific computing has a workforce problem: the people who could benefit most from supercomputers often never encounter one. The **DOE Intro to HPC Bootcamp**, organized with the Sustainable Horizons Institute and the DOE computing facilities, introduces undergraduates from new and underrepresented communities to HPC through project-based work on energy-justice questions. I have served as a mentor and project lead in 2023 and 2025; my 2025 project had students use large language models to generate and critically evaluate HPC teaching material.

**AI for Energy Justice** grew out of the bootcamp: tutorials on machine learning, LLMs, web scraping, and fine-tuning, and the AI-Justice project that scores renewable-energy legislation against the Initiative for Energy Justice scorecard. **FireAID** (DOE/ASCR, 2025–2028), for which I am the Argonne lead PI, is an undergraduate research training program that develops AI and deep-learning technologies for fighting wildland fire in Alaska.

Closer to home: mentoring for the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy's Student Inquiry and Research program, Sustainable Research Pathways students, Introduce a Girl to Engineering Day, annual Hour-of-Code lessons at local schools since 2019, and judging middle-school science fairs since 2018.
